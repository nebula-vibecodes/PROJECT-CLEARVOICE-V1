---
name: Home symbol audit
description: Criteria for assigning Mulberry artwork to Clearvoice Home vocabulary.
---

Use direct household-object or home-activity artwork for Home tiles. A clean-room depiction is a clear match for Clean, while a cooker is not a substitute for the Kitchen room. Keep compound requests such as wanting to sit here, asking for help, or wanting something here text-only when a base action would omit important context.

**Why:** Home vocabulary mixes objects, rooms, actions, and requests. Associating a related object or partial action with a room or compound request can change the AAC meaning.

**How to apply:** Prefer an exact room/object label or a visually clear household activity. Reuse an existing local Mulberry asset before adding another SVG, and preserve the complete spoken phrase for compound tiles.
