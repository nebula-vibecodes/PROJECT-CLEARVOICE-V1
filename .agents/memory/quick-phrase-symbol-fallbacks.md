---
name: Quick Phrase symbol fallbacks
description: The semantic rule for mapping Clearvoice Quick Phrases to existing Mulberry assets.
---

`Yes, please` may reuse the direct yes symbol and `No, thank you` may reuse the direct no symbol because politeness does not change the central AAC response. Keep `Please stop`, `I don't know`, `I'm uncomfortable`, `Not that`, `Say it again`, `Slower, please`, and `I am hurt` text-based unless an exact official symbol becomes available.

**Why:** The two yes/no phrases preserve an unmistakable central response, while the other phrases combine a nuanced action, uncertainty, emotion, repetition, pacing, or health meaning that a nearby symbol would only partially communicate.

**How to apply:** Compare the full practical phrase meaning before reusing a symbol. Do not increase coverage by mapping a related keyword when it could create ambiguity.
