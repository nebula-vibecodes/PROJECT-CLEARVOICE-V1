---
name: Activities symbol audit
description: Criteria for assigning Mulberry artwork to Clearvoice Activities vocabulary.
---

Use Mulberry artwork for concrete Activities actions when the catalog symbol directly depicts the complete word or phrase: play, read, watch, music, draw, walk, rest, go outside, wait, and finish are clear matches. Keep Listen, My turn, Start, and compound requests text-only when available symbols would omit context or introduce a different meaning.

**Why:** Small AAC tiles need artwork that remains immediately recognizable and semantically faithful. A partial symbol can be less understandable than a deliberate text fallback.

**How to apply:** Review both catalog metadata and the rendered tile at its actual size. Prefer exact action or noun depictions; do not substitute generic turn, time, join, change, question, or directional symbols for compound activity phrases.
