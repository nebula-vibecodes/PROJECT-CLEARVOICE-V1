name: School/work symbol audit
description: Durable matching rules for School/work AAC vocabulary and official Mulberry artwork.
---

For School/work vocabulary, use official Mulberry art only when the pictured classroom, object, action, or concept directly matches the tile. The approved direct matches are classroom, desktop computer, paper, finished, desk, write, ready, and show me. Keep teacher, coworker, meeting, task, read this, explain that, different task, and what comes next text-only when the available artwork adds identity or omits meaningful phrase context.

**Why:** A visually related symbol can change an AAC message when it introduces an unspecified person or drops the request target, deictic context, or question meaning.

**How to apply:** Prefer exact catalog labels and neutral variants; reuse a symbol already approved elsewhere when the meaning is identical, and preserve the text fallback when no direct match exists.
