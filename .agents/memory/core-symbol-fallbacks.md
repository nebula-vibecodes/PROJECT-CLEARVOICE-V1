---
name: Core symbol fallbacks
description: The semantic and safety rule for the remaining Clearvoice Core concepts without direct Mulberry matches.
---

The Core concepts `i`, `you`, `need`, `stop`, `not`, `that`, `it`, `feel`, `can`, `do`, and `again` remain text-based unless the official Mulberry catalog gains a direct, non-misleading match. Nearby concepts such as finish, enough, wrong, or same should not be substituted.

**Why:** In AAC, an approximate image can communicate a different word or action. The official catalog audit found no exact stop or again symbol, and the other remaining concepts are pronouns, modifiers, or abstract words whose available images would change the intended meaning.

**How to apply:** During future Mulberry audits, preserve these as intentional text fallbacks and only add an asset after checking the full spoken meaning, not just a related keyword.
