---
name: Mulberry phrase aliases
description: When a phrase can safely reuse an existing concrete Mulberry symbol without losing AAC meaning.
---

A polite modifier such as “please” may share the symbol for the phrase’s concrete base concept when it does not add a distinct AAC meaning. Modifiers that add duration, location, uncertainty, intensity, or another meaningful relation should remain fallbacks unless an exact symbol exists. Action-specific requests such as “call my caregiver” must not use a role or support image that omits the requested action.

**Why:** A symbol for “more” or “help” still communicates the core request in “more, please” or “help, please,” while symbols for “hungry,” “outside,” or “look” would not fully communicate phrases such as “still hungry,” “go outside,” or “read this.”

**How to apply:** During Mulberry audits, compare the full spoken meaning—not only the keyword—and reuse an existing asset only for semantically preserving politeness or another non-substantive modifier.
