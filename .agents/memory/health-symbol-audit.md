---
name: Health symbol audit
description: Durable criteria for matching Mulberry artwork to urgent and physical-needs vocabulary.
---

Approved direct Health matches are medicine, breathing via airflow artwork, rest, head, stomach, and the existing water mapping. The four original Clearvoice urgent pictograms remain unchanged. Keep Doctor, Nurse, Sick, Dizzy, Fever, and compound care or symptom phrases text-only when available art is gendered, a different symptom, an instrument, or incomplete for the full message.

**Why:** Health symbols can affect how quickly and accurately a person communicates a physical need. A nearby medical image is not safe if it changes the symptom, urgency, body location, or request meaning.

**How to apply:** Prefer neutral, recognizable art tested at tile size. Never replace an original urgent pictogram without proving equal urgency and clearer communication, and preserve urgent labels, announcements, IDs, positions, and interaction behavior.
