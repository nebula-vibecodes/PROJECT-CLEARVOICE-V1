---
name: People symbol audit
description: Criteria for assigning Mulberry artwork to Clearvoice People vocabulary.
---

Use an official Mulberry symbol for a People tile only when the artwork directly communicates the full label without adding gender, age, appearance, family-structure, plurality, or relationship assumptions. Keep a text fallback when the closest symbol is gendered, depicts a different relationship, or only covers part of a compound phrase.

**Why:** AAC symbols can change the meaning of a person or relationship label even when the keyword looks close. Text preserves the intended vocabulary concept when Mulberry has no neutral exact match.

**How to apply:** Check the full label and spoken output against the catalog entry. Prefer direct relationship or communication symbols such as Family, Hello, and Please wait; do not substitute parent/child, sibling gender variants, a generic profession depiction, or a partial action for a more specific People concept.
