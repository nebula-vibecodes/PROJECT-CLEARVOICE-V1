---
name: Food symbol audit
description: Criteria for assigning Mulberry artwork to Clearvoice Food vocabulary.
---

Use a Mulberry food symbol only when it directly represents the full Food label. Keep Snack text-only when the catalog offers only specific snack foods or meals, and keep longer requests or qualifiers text-only when a base symbol would omit their meaning. Exact state concepts such as All done and I am full may use the matching Mulberry symbols.

**Why:** Visually similar food pictures are not interchangeable AAC concepts, and partial symbols can make a request sound more specific or less specific than intended.

**How to apply:** Compare the complete label and spoken output with the Mulberry catalog name and depiction. Reuse an existing symbol only when it preserves the whole concept, not merely one keyword in a compound phrase.
