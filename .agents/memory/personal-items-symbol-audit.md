For Personal items, approved direct matches are generic mobile phone, glasses, bag via the existing handbag asset, key, toys, electric charger, headphones, and wallet. Keep Tablet, Shoes, Book, and all request, location, use, nearby, and ownership phrases text-only when the closest art is narrower, gendered, medical, or omits the phrase context.

**Why:** A concrete item symbol should remain neutral and recognizable without adding a brand, school context, gender, medical meaning, or an ownership/request assumption.

**How to apply:** Prefer generic object artwork at tile size, reuse an existing approved asset when it remains semantically suitable, and do not map action or communication-device art to a broader possession label.
