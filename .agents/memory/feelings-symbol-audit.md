---
name: Feelings symbol audit
description: Durable semantic and neutrality rules for Clearvoice emotion vocabulary.
---

For unqualified Feelings tiles, use Mulberry artwork only when it is a neutral, readable, direct representation of the exact feeling or complete phrase. Gendered emotion faces and nearby actions such as relaxing, sleeping, laughing, being quiet, or talking are not safe substitutions. The remaining Feelings fallback set intentionally stays text-only; the existing Confused mapping is preserved.

**Why:** Emotional AAC errors can change the user’s intended meaning, and a gendered or action-based image adds information or emotion context the tile does not say.

**How to apply:** Audit each feeling independently rather than grouping positive, negative, or arousal-related emotions. Preserve text fallback for calm, tired, hurt, uncomfortable, okay, proud, unsure, overwhelmed, and similar concepts unless a neutral exact symbol is available.
